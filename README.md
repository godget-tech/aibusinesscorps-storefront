## What Is This?

This is an autonomous AI system that:
1. 🔍 **Scans** for digital product opportunities
2. 🏗️ **Creates** products (code tools, templates, content packs)
3. ✅ **Reviews** quality automatically
4. 🛒 **Lists** them for sale
5. 💰 **Tracks** earnings
6. 🧠 **Learns** from what works

**No human in the loop. It just runs.**

## Architecture

```
Money Engine (Python)
├── Opportunity Scanner — Finds market opportunities
├── Product Generator — Creates code/content/templates  
├── Quality Reviewer — Validates every deliverable
├── Evolution Tracker — Learns from success/failure patterns
└── Stats Reporter — Live dashboard of earnings
```

Built on Corellis multi-agent engine with Evolution tracking.
